# Nine Men's Morris — BOARDORA Rule Specification

## Board & Pieces
- **Board**: Three concentric squares linked by four midpoint radial lines, forming **24 intersection points**.
- **Pieces**: Two players (**Ivory Gold** vs **Obsidian**) with **9 pieces each**.

## Three Phases of Play
1. **Placement Phase**: Players alternate placing 1 piece onto any empty intersection until all 18 pieces have been placed.
2. **Movement Phase**: Once all 9 pieces are on the board, players slide 1 piece along any board line to an adjacent empty point.
3. **Flying Phase**: When a player is reduced to exactly **3 pieces**, they may "fly" any piece to **any** empty point on the board.

## Mills & Capturing
- Forming three of your pieces in a continuous straight line (a **Mill**) immediately grants a capture of one opponent piece.
- You may not capture an opponent piece that is currently inside a mill, **unless** all of the opponent's remaining pieces are in mills.

## Winning & Draw Conditions
- **Win by Reduction**: Reduce your opponent to **2 pieces** (fewer than 3 remaining after placement).
- **Win by Blockade**: Leave your opponent with **zero legal moves** at the start of their turn.
- **Draw**: Threefold board repetition or 50 consecutive movement plies with no capture.
